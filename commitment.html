Our commitment

How we build.

Our mission, our pledge to the people who buy our software, and the good-faith terms behind everything we ship.

Mission

SIM DAD builds local-first software for serious work: tools that run on your own machine, keep your data yours, and earn trust through what they actually do rather than what they claim. We make software for researchers, writers, clinicians, journalists, and anyone whose work is too important, or too private, to hand to someone else's cloud. We build carefully, in the open, and over time, and we measure ourselves by one thing: whether the people who rely on our tools can keep relying on them.

Our pledge

The tool you buy today is the floor, not the ceiling. We ship updates continuously, and we do our best to deliver every capability we describe.

Some features you read about are available now; some are in active development or on our near-term roadmap. We try to always say which is which, and we label what is planned rather than shipped. We keep a dated development record, so our word is backed by evidence, not just intentions.

If we describe something and then genuinely cannot deliver it, we will tell you plainly and make it right. Your data stays yours, on your machine. This is a small company building carefully, and we take your confidence seriously.
