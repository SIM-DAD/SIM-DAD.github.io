SIUE Professor Launches No-Code Text Analysis Tool for Researchers

TASS offers dictionary-based scoring, statistical comparisons and publication-ready exports without requiring programming skills or cloud accounts

GRANITE CITY, Ill. — SIM DAD LLC today announced the public release of TASS (Text Analysis Support Software), a desktop application that enables social scientists, journalists and domain experts to conduct dictionary-based text analysis without writing code or uploading data to external servers.

TASS is the first commercially available text analysis tool that combines dictionary scoring, inferential statistics and APA-formatted exports in a single offline application, according to its creator, Alex Leith, PhD, an associate professor of mass communications at Southern Illinois University Edwardsville.

"I built TASS because I watched my students spend more time debugging R scripts than analyzing their data," Leith said. "Researchers shouldn't need a computer science degree to run a sentiment analysis."

The application supports Welch's t-test, Mann-Whitney U, one-way ANOVA, Kruskal-Wallis, Tukey HSD and Dunn's post-hoc comparisons, Pearson and Spearman correlations, TF-IDF scoring and n-gram analysis. All statistical outputs include effect sizes, confidence intervals and assumption checks. Results export as APA-formatted Excel tables with a reproducibility metadata sidecar documenting every analytical decision.

TASS runs entirely on the user's Windows computer. No data is transmitted after installation, and no internet connection is required for analysis — a design decision Leith said reflects the realities of working with IRB-protected data and student records.

"When a researcher is analyzing focus group transcripts or survey responses covered by an IRB protocol, the question isn't whether a cloud provider's privacy policy is adequate," Leith said. "The question is whether the data needs to leave the building at all. With TASS, it doesn't."

The application is available through Gumroad with a 30-day free trial. Subscription pricing starts at $79 per year for academic users, with lab and department tiers available for institutional adoption. All tiers include the full feature set.

TASS was developed over two years as part of SIM DAD LLC, a software company Leith founded in 2024 to build local-first tools for knowledge workers. The company's other products include Ibis, a local audio transcription application, and Lector, a read-aloud editing tool.

For more information, visit sim-dad.github.io or contact ap@simdadllc.com.

About SIM DAD LLC

SIM DAD LLC is a Granite City, Illinois-based software company that builds local-first desktop tools for researchers, educators and knowledge workers. Founded in 2024 by Alex Leith, PhD, the company develops applications that process all data on the user's hardware with no cloud dependency. Products include TASS (text analysis), Ibis (audio transcription), Lector (read-aloud editing) and Orator (document-to-video narration). More information is available at sim-dad.github.io.
