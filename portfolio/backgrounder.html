About SIM DAD

SIM DAD LLC is a software company that builds local-first desktop tools for researchers, educators, and knowledge workers. Founded in June 2024 by Alex Leith, PhD, the company develops applications that run entirely on the user's machine — no cloud accounts, no subscriptions, no data leaving the device. SIM DAD is headquartered in Granite City, Illinois, and distributes its products through Gumroad with one-time or annual pricing.

The company's founding philosophy reflects a gap its founder identified through more than a decade of academic work: researchers and educators routinely depend on cloud-based tools that introduce data privacy risks, recurring costs, and platform lock-in. SIM DAD's product line eliminates these dependencies by delivering professional-grade software that operates offline after installation (SIM DAD LLC, 2024).

Products

TASS (Text Analysis Support Software) is a Windows desktop application for dictionary-based text scoring, group comparison, and publication-ready statistical exports. Designed for social scientists, journalists, and domain experts with no coding background, TASS provides the analytical capabilities of tools like LIWC-22 without requiring a programming environment. TASS supports Welch's t-test, Mann-Whitney U, ANOVA, Kruskal-Wallis, Tukey HSD and Dunn's post-hoc tests, Spearman and Pearson correlations, TF-IDF scoring, and n-gram analysis. The application exports APA-formatted tables and includes a reproducibility metadata sidecar for transparent reporting. TASS is scheduled for public release on May 31, 2026, with subscription pricing starting at $79 per year for academic users (SIM DAD LLC, 2026).

Ibis is a Windows desktop application that monitors a folder and automatically transcribes audio files using OpenAI's Whisper large-v3-turbo model running locally. Ibis processes MP3, WAV, M4A, and other common audio formats without sending data to external servers. Pricing is one-time: $24 (Personal) or $49 (Pro) through Gumroad (SIM DAD LLC, 2025).

Lector is a local web application combining a distraction-free Markdown editor with word-by-word text-to-speech and XTTS-v2 voice cloning. The application is priced at $29 one-time for up to three devices (SIM DAD LLC, 2025).

Orator is a document-to-video pipeline that converts Markdown and Quarto documents into narrated, camera-ready MP4 videos using on-device voice cloning via F5-TTS. Orator targets educators, developer advocates, and corporate training teams who write before they present. The product is currently in internal testing and is scheduled for public release in December 2026 (SIM DAD LLC, 2026).

Leadership

Alex Leith, PhD, is the founder and sole operator of SIM DAD LLC. He is an Associate Professor of Mass Communications at Southern Illinois University Edwardsville, where he directs the Social Interaction and Media (SIM) Lab. His research focuses on social virtual reality, media psychology, and human-computer interaction. He has published 24 peer-reviewed works in outlets including Communication Monographs, Computers in Human Behavior Reports, IEEE Transactions on Games, and PLOS One, and served as Co-PI on a $1.59 million National Science Foundation grant studying virtual meeting technologies (Leith, 2024).

Local-First Philosophy

Every SIM DAD product adheres to three principles: (1) all processing occurs on the user's hardware, (2) no user data is transmitted after installation, and (3) no internet connection is required for core functionality. This approach addresses documented concerns about cloud-based research tools and institutional data policies, particularly in fields that handle IRB-protected data, student records, or proprietary organizational information (SIM DAD LLC, 2024).
