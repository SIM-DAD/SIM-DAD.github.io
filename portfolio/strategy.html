Lab 4 — Step 3: Organizing

Strategic Message Planner

Campaign goals, key messages, tactics and timeline.

Client: SIM DAD LLC

Campaign: TASS v1.0 Product Launch

Prepared by: Alex Leith, PhD

Date: Feb. 13, 2026

Campaign Goals

  • Awareness: Generate 25 earned media mentions or coverage placements in education technology and academic research publications within 90 days of launch (May 31-Aug. 31, 2026).
  • Conversion: Achieve 200 paid subscriptions (any tier) within 60 days of launch.
  • Positioning: Establish TASS as the primary alternative to LIWC-22 for researchers who do not code, as measured by branded search volume and community discussion mentions.

Target Audience

Primary: Social science researchers and graduate students at U.S. universities who conduct dictionary-based text analysis but lack programming skills. Aged 25-45. Use tools like LIWC, SPSS or Excel for analysis. Follow methods scholars on Twitter/X and subscribe to discipline-specific listservs. (See Lab 2: Audience Persona — Dr. Maya Chen.)

Secondary: Journalism professors and newsroom analysts who analyze media coverage patterns. Communication department chairs evaluating site-license alternatives to LIWC-22.

Key Messages

  • "Your data deserves better tools." TASS delivers the analytical power of LIWC-22 without requiring a cloud account, a subscription renewal or a single line of code.
  • "Built by a researcher, for researchers." TASS was designed by a professor who teaches research methods — every feature reflects the actual workflow of academic text analysis.
  • "Local-first means your data stays yours." Every analysis runs on the user's machine. No data is transmitted. No internet is required. IRB-protected and proprietary data stays where it belongs.
