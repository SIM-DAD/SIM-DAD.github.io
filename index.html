Who we are

About SIM DAD LLC.

SIM DAD LLC is a research and consulting practice specializing in social and interactive media, human-computer interaction, and large-scale behavioral data analysis. We work with organizations, research teams, and program officers who need rigorous methods and the scale to match — from study design through publication-ready findings.

Our approach combines academic research rigor with applied delivery. We design studies, run pipelines across hundreds of millions of records, and produce findings that hold up to peer review. We have managed cross-institutional research programs, built formal data-access partnerships with major technology companies, and led grant-funded work from proposal through publication.

The software portfolio — Ibis, TASS, and Lector — grew directly from our own research needs. These are tools we built for ourselves, now available commercially. See Products below.

Software & Projects

These tools were built to solve real research problems. They are now available commercially. Local-first. One-time pricing where possible. Built to the standard of tools we would use ourselves.

01
Ibis
Local AI · Windows Desktop · v1.0 Complete · Launching soon — $24

Ibis watches a folder on your Windows PC and transcribes every audio file automatically using on-device AI. Powered by Whisper large-v3-turbo. 99 languages. No cloud. No subscription. One-time purchase. v1.0 is built and in pre-launch.

Product site →
02
TASS
Text Analysis · Windows Desktop · Launching May 2026 — $49/yr

TASS is a native Windows desktop app for dictionary-based text analysis — no Python, R, or command line required. Features group comparisons (t-test, ANOVA), publication-quality visualizations, and APA citation blocks built in. Targets launch May 2026.

Product site →
03
Lector
Writing Tool · Windows · Coming soon — $29

Lector is a distraction-free writing environment with read-aloud built in. Write your draft, then hear it read back word by word. Voice cloning via F5-TTS. Completely local — no cloud processing, no account required.

Product site →
04
Orator
Video Pipeline · Windows · Coming soon — $79

Orator turns markdown documents into narrated, camera-ready videos using local voice cloning. One command: parse, narrate, render slides, compose MP4. Your voice and your content never leave your machine. No cloud APIs, no subscription.
